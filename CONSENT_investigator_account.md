### Standing limits that survive this authorisation

These are analytical, not permissions, and they travel with the account wherever
it is used:

1. **n=1.** It does not establish a population rate. The "100%" formulation is his
   assessment, not a finding, and must never be presented as established.
2. **Toxicology cannot distinguish 7-OH product from leaf kratom.** Product
   identity rests on what he took, not on the assay.
3. **THC must be disclosed** wherever the account is used. Omitting it and having
   it surface later would discredit the whole thing.
4. **He is not a disinterested party** and any use should say so.
