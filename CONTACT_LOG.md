# CASE SEVENFOLD / CONTACT LOG

Every approach this investigation has made to a public official or agency, what
channel it used, and what came back.

**Why this file exists.** People are entitled to see how the offices that
represent them actually respond when a constituent brings them documented work.
Not a characterisation of it. The record of it.

**How to read it.** This log is deliberately flat. No adjectives, no
interpretation, no complaint. Every row is a date, a channel, and an outcome. Some
offices respond quickly. Some route you to a form. Some are legally obliged to
answer and some are not, and that distinction explains most of what you will see
below. **Draw your own conclusions. That is the point of publishing it.**

Nothing here alleges wrongdoing by any office. Routing a caller to a web form is
ordinary practice, not misconduct.

## What the log shows so far

**Response time is not about how good your material is. It is about whether the
office has a duty to answer.**

- The **state consumer protection division**, which has a statutory function and a
  ticketing system, acknowledged in **23 seconds** with a reference number.
- The **federal docket**, where comments are part of a rulemaking record, issued a
  **tracking number for every submission**.
- **FDA** refused a request on a channel technicality but **wrote back and told us
  the correct route**, which is a real answer.
- The **Senate personal office** has no obligation to read, respond, or act. Its
  web form issues **no reference number of any kind**, so a constituent cannot
  later prove what they sent or follow up on it. Its published phone line routes
  callers back to that same form.

That last point is the one worth sitting with. A member of the public who wants to
raise something documented with their own senator has exactly one channel, it
returns no receipt, and the phone number exists to point you back to it.

**We are not saying anyone did anything wrong.** We are saying this is what the
machinery does, and most people never see it because most people only ever try
once.

## Method

Every row is verified, not assumed:

- Email delivery is confirmed against the **Sent folder over IMAP** and against
  **bounce messages**, never against an SMTP acceptance. SMTP accepting a message
  proves handoff, not delivery.
- A submission enters the FILED section of `FILINGS.md` **only** with an agency
  acknowledgment, a tracking number, or a docket receipt. This rule was added
  after an FDA FOIA sat recorded as "sent" for a day when the agency had in fact
  refused it.
- Phone contacts are recorded as **[S] the investigator's contemporaneous
  account**, and labelled as such, because we did not record the calls.

Corrections are welcome and will be published. If an office believes a row here is
inaccurate, we will amend it and say that we did.
