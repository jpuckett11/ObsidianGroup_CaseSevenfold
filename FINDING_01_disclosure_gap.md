### 3. Why the record has a hole in it, structurally

The top entry in his Senate filing list is:

> **"Annual Report for 2025 Due Date Extension 1" — filed 2026-03-16**

There is **no CY2025 annual report**. He took the extension on 2026-03-16 and was
confirmed as Secretary of Homeland Security on **2026-03-23**, seven days later.

**[I]** The Senate disclosure that would have covered a calendar-year-2025
acquisition was deferred and then never filed, because he left the chamber. The
holding surfaced publicly only because a *different* system, OGE's nominee
process, has different requirements and caught it.

### 4. What this does and does not establish

**Establishes:**
- He holds $500,001-$1,000,000 in a leaf-kratom manufacturer, directly.
- That holding was excluded from a 63-equity, 19-bond divestiture agreement that
  captured even Philip Morris International.
- He has certified completion of all required divestitures while retaining it.
- No Senate filing at any point disclosed it.

**Does NOT establish:**
- **The acquisition date.** Bounded to **2025-01-01 through 2026-03-17** on the
  documents in hand. The 2025-07-29 podium appearance falls inside that window.
  We cannot presently say whether he owned it that day. **Anyone asserting he did
  is going beyond the record.**
> **UPDATED 2026-08-27, see Finding 10.** The reporting-violation line of
> reasoning is substantially WITHDRAWN. All 43 PTRs were parsed: every
> transaction in them is a public security or municipal bond, and none of his
> dozen-plus private LLCs has ever appeared in a PTR. Private-company
> interests are evidently not treated as PTR-reportable by his office, so the
> absence of Botanic Tonics there is normal practice, not concealment.

- **A reporting violation.** Three innocent explanations survive and must be
  eliminated before any such claim is made:
  1. Acquired in 2025, properly disclosable only on the CY2025 annual that his
     departure mooted.
  2. A private LLC membership interest may not be PTR-reportable under the STOCK
     Act the way a listed security is. This needs a straight answer, not our guess.
  3. Agency ethics counsel may have determined recusal rather than divestiture was
     the appropriate remedy. Section 3 of the agreement shows that determination
     being made explicitly for **Apres Spectrum LLC**, so the mechanism exists and
     was used elsewhere in this very document. **If it was applied to Botanic
     Tonics, it is not written down anywhere in the agreement.**

**[I]** Explanation 3 is the most important thread left. The agreement openly
records "the agency has determined that it is not necessary at this time for me to
divest" for another holding. That language does not appear for Botanic Tonics.
The company is simply absent.
