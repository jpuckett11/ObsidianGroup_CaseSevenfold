### 4. Where the product comes from

- **Leaf.** Over 90% of world kratom supply is Indonesian, principally West
  Kalimantan and Sumatra. [S]
- **7-OH.** Occurs in the leaf only in trace amounts, too little to isolate
  economically. Commercial 7-OH is **semi-synthetic**: bulk purified mitragynine
  is bought by the kilogram and a hydroxyl group is added at the 7 position in a
  lab, frequently in unregulated facilities. [S] peer-reviewed
- **[I]** Both sides of this commercial fight run on the same Indonesian raw
  material. The dispute is over which downstream processing step is lawful. The
  0.05% threshold is, in effect, a rule about who is allowed to do chemistry to a
  shared input.
- **Gap:** no primary evidence yet on Botanic Tonics' own importer or supplier.
  Customs/bill-of-lading data would settle it.
