# SEVENFOLD / FINDING 07
## Who the enforcement actually hit, and when

**Status:** CONFIRMED from FDA primary. `exhibits/fda_7oh_warning_letters_pr.*`

### The seven firms

FDA announced seven warning letters on **2025-07-15**:

1. Shaman Botanicals, LLC
2. My Smoke Wholesale
3. Relax Relief Rejuvenate Trading, LLC dba RRR Trading / EDP Kratom
4. **Thang Botanicals, Inc. dba 7ΩHMZ, 7-OHMZ, or 7OHMZ**
5. Royal Diamond Imports, Inc. dba Roxytabs.com
6. Hydroxie, LLC
7. 7Tabz Retail, LLC

Recipients were given 15 business days to respond.

FDA's stated basis: 7-OH is not lawful in dietary supplements, cannot lawfully be
added to conventional foods, and no FDA-approved drug contains it. The letters
target "concentrated 7-OH products such as tablets, gummies, drink mixes, and
shots," expressly noting 7-OH "occurs naturally in trace amounts in kratom."

### Two dates that matter

- **2025-07-15** FDA issues the seven letters.
- **2025-07-29** RFK Jr., Makary and Sen. Mullin hold the press conference.

**[I]** The enforcement action preceded the political event by two weeks. The
press conference was staged on top of an action already taken, which is
consistent with the campaign seeking visibility and momentum rather than
originating the enforcement.

### Not on the list

**No leaf-kratom product received a warning letter in this action.** Not Botanic
Tonics, not Johnson Foods, not MIT45's leaf lines.

**[I]** Consistent with a genuine concentrate-versus-leaf distinction, and equally
consistent with the commercial outcome the leaf incumbents were lobbying for.
The enforcement record alone does not distinguish between those readings.
