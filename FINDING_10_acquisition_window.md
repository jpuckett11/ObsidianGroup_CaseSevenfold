### 2. CORRECTION to Finding 01

Finding 01 listed "a reporting violation" as a live possibility because Botanic
Tonics appears in none of Mullin's periodic transaction reports.

**That reasoning was weak and is now substantially withdrawn.**

We pulled and parsed the transaction tables of **all 43 PTRs** filed from October
2023 through March 2026. **Every single transaction in every one of them is a
publicly traded stock, ETF, mutual fund, or municipal security.** Not one private
LLC, partnership interest or private-placement transaction appears anywhere in
three years of filings.

Mullin's OGE disclosure shows he holds at least a dozen private LLCs (Mullin
Properties, Mullin Realty, Mullin Ranch, Mullin Properties West Division,
Allotment Services, Apres Spectrum, Circle 6 Rodeo, Mullin Family Holdco, TC4
Investments, Mullin Family Investments, K5 HS Holdco). **None of those has ever
appeared in a PTR either.**

**[I]** His office does not treat private-company interests as PTR-reportable. The
absence of Botanic Tonics from the PTRs is therefore **normal practice, not
evidence of concealment,** and must not be characterised as a violation.

### 4. The hypothesis, labelled as such

**[I] Most consistent with all available evidence:** Mullin acquired his interest
in the **final tranche of this offering, between 2025-01-01 and 2025-01-07**, or
by **secondary transfer after the round closed**.

Reasoning:
- If he were among the **original 28** investors (on or before 2024-11-21), the
  interest would have been held at 2024 year end and belonged on the CY2024
  annual, where it is absent.
- If he were among the **two investors added by 2025-01-07**, those two together
  put in $22,572,324. His band is $500,001-$1,000,000, so he could only be the
  smaller of the pair, against a partner of roughly $22M. **Arithmetically
  possible, not demonstrated.**
- A purchase dated **2025-01-01 to 2025-01-07** falls outside CY2024 and inside the
  CY2025 annual that was never filed. **That single fact would explain the entire
  disclosure gap without any misconduct in the filings themselves.**

**This remains inference. We have no document placing Mullin in this offering.**
Do not write that he invested in this round. Write that the company's only
registered offering ran 2024-11-07 to 2025-01-07, and that his acquisition date is
undisclosed.
