### 0. Read this before quoting any number below

These come from **FDA's CFSAN Adverse Event Reporting System (CAERS)**. CAERS is
**voluntary, unverified, and self-reported**. A "Death" outcome means a report
described a death alongside use of the product. **It does not mean the product
caused the death.** Reports are also heavily influenced by publicity, litigation
and reporting drives.

Used honestly, this is still the federal government's own record of what consumers
and clinicians told it. Used dishonestly, it is junk. **Every OWG artifact citing
these numbers must carry this paragraph.**

### 5. IMPORTANT counter-evidence on the scheduling asymmetry

We have argued that leaving mitragynine unscheduled is convenient for the
incumbents. **There is a legitimate competing explanation and it is strong.**

**Mitragynine now holds Investigational New Drug status.** NIDA is sponsoring
**MG001**, a purified mitragynine formulation, in the first human study of
mitragynine under an FDA-regulated drug development programme: Phase 1, 32 healthy
volunteers, 25-100 mg single ascending doses, beginning September 2026, targeted
at **opioid use disorder**. (NCT07204171. Developed by NIH/NIDA with University of
Florida, **not** Sparian Biosciences, which is developing the separate SBS-147.)

**[I]** Placing mitragynine in Schedule I would obstruct an active federal drug
development programme aimed at opioid addiction. That is a coherent, non-corrupt
reason for the asymmetry, and any OWG output must state it. **Do not write that
mitragynine was spared to protect Mullin's investment.**

**But it does not transfer to the warnings.** A compound undergoing Phase 1 safety
testing is not a demonstrated-safe consumer product. An active IND is an argument
for *more* consumer caution about unregulated retail leaf, not less. The warning
removals remain undefended.

### 7. For people who have been harmed

Practical, and worth carrying in any public artifact:

- **Report an adverse event to FDA**: safetyreporting.hhs.gov, or 1-800-FDA-1088.
  CAERS only reflects what gets reported, and under-reporting is the norm.
- **Poison control, 24/7**: 1-800-222-1222.
- **SAMHSA national helpline**, free and confidential, 24/7: 1-800-662-HELP (4357).
- ***In re Botanic Tonics Litigation***, N.D. Cal. 3:23-cv-01460-VC, covered US
  purchasers of Feel Free between 2019-03-28 and 2025-03-05. Settlement
  administration at feelfreeclassaction.com. **Verify current claim deadlines
  before telling anyone they can still file.**
- **The HHS comment docket on the 7-OH threshold is open until 2026-09-10.**
  Members of the public can file. Comments are public and go to the Secretary of
  HHS for the Attorney General's consideration.
