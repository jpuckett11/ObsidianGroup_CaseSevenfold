### 1. The $22.57M investors: CLOSED, not publicly discoverable

Finding 10 established that Botanic Tonics' only registered offering ran
2024-11-07 to 2025-01-07, and that **$22,572,324 arrived from two investors**
between 2024-11-21 and 2025-01-07.

**Those investors cannot be identified from public records, and no further
searching will change that.** Regulation D Form D discloses the offering, the
amount, the investor count and the issuer's related persons. **It never names
purchasers.** There is no state analogue that does, no SEC follow-on filing for a
private issuer, and no beneficial-ownership disclosure that reaches an
accredited-investor placement of this size.

**[I] This thread reopens only through litigation discovery, a subpoena, a
regulatory demand, or a person who chooses to talk.** We record it as closed
rather than leaving it as an open task that will never resolve.

### 2. LD-203 lobbying contribution reports: pulled, structurally limited

The Senate LDA contributions endpoint was queried for every firm in the case. It
had not previously been touched by this investigation.

| Firm | LD-203 records |
|---|---|
| Holland & Knight | 2,333 |
| McGuireWoods Consulting | 677 |
| Miller Strategies | 556 |
| Troutman Strategies | 215 |
| Checkmate Government Relations | 25 |

**[I] LD-203 cannot answer who funds a client.** The form reports political
contributions by a *registrant firm and its individual lobbyists*, aggregated
across the firm's entire book and its full filing history. **Contributions are not
attributed to clients.** So it cannot connect Miller Strategies' giving to
Botanicals for Better Health and Wellness, or Checkmate's to Stop Gas Station
Heroin. This is a limit of the disclosure regime, not of the search.

One item worth noting rather than concluding from: **Checkmate Government
Relations' LD-203 records show $10,050 to "McDowell for Congress."** Checkmate's
principal is **Charles "Ches" McDowell**. Whether that is the same family is
**unverified** and we make no claim about it.

Historical context from the same pull, all firm-wide and spanning decades:
Troutman's largest single line is **$1,500,000 to the Next Century Fund**; Holland
& Knight's largest are party committees on both sides (DSCC $85,500, NRCC
$75,000, DCCC $50,750); McGuireWoods' is **$100,500 to Montanans for Tester**.
None of this is kratom-attributable and none of it should be presented as such.
