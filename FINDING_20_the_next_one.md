# SEVENFOLD / FINDING 20
## 9-hydroxycorynantheidine: the scaffold the scheduling order does not reach

**Status:** CONFIRMED, with controls, from federal primaries.
**Date:** 2026-08-28. **Opened at the investigator's direction.**

---

### 1. What it is

**9-hydroxycorynantheidine (9-OH-corynantheidine)** is a minor indole alkaloid of
*Mitragyna speciosa*, comprising **less than 1% of total alkaloid content** in the
plant. It is a **partial agonist at the mu-opioid receptor**, demonstrated in
guinea-pig ileum, with weaker mu affinity than 7-OH. [S] peer-reviewed

**[I] Structurally it stands to the corynantheidine scaffold as 7-OH stands to
mitragynine: a hydroxylated minor alkaloid with mu-opioid activity, present in the
plant in trace amounts too small to extract economically.** The same economics that
made commercial 7-OH a semi-synthetic product apply here.

### 2. It is not covered by any federal scheduling action

| Instrument | Covers |
|---|---|
| DEA proposed rule, 2026-07-06 (DEA-2026-1190) | 7-OH above a threshold |
| DEA final rule, 2026-08-26 (FR 2026-17429) | mitragynine pseudoindoxyl, **MGM-15** (dihydro-7-hydroxymitragynine), **MGM-16** (9-fluoro variant) |
| HHS letter to DEA, 2026-03-06 | 7-OH only, by percentage and by 1.00 mg absolute |

**All four scheduled substances sit on the mitragynine scaffold. Nothing on the
corynantheidine scaffold is covered.**

### 4. But a state already covered it

**Florida's emergency rule of June 2026 names 9-hydroxycorynantheidine as one of
seven covered compounds.** [S]

**[I] A state regulator identified this compound and wrote it into a rule. The
federal action, issued weeks later, did not.**

### 5. DEA anticipated substitution and covered the wrong scaffold

From the final rule, DEA's own reasoning for scheduling MGM-16:

> "While no evidence supports the presence of MGM-16 on the *Mitragyna speciosa*
> consumer market, its profile as a highly potent opioid that is structurally
> related to 7-hydroxymitragynine lends itself as an [alternative]..."

**[I] DEA scheduled a compound that is not on the market, preemptively, on the
explicit reasoning that sellers would substitute toward structurally related
opioids. That reasoning is correct. It was applied only within one chemical
family.** The same logic reaches 9-OH-corynantheidine and was not extended to it.

### 6. There is no money on it yet, and that is the point

The investigator asked whether the funding pattern matches 7-OH. **It does not,
because it has not started.** Zero lobbying filings. Zero docket activity. Zero
adverse event reports.

**[I] This compound is ahead of the money.** Every other finding in this case
documents an influence operation after the fact. This one is a window before it.
The commercial incentive to move onto an unscheduled mu-opioid alkaloid from the
same plant, using the same supply chain, the same retail channel and the same
"botanical" framing, is obvious and immediate the day 7-OH scheduling takes
effect.

### 7. What is NOT established

- **We have not found 9-OH-corynantheidine products for sale.** Absence of search
  results is weak evidence in a market that renames constantly. Not asserted
  either way.
- **No patent search has been run** on synthesis routes. That is the single
  strongest forward indicator, since a granted process patent for 7-OH existed
  before enforcement. **Do this next.**
- **Its potency relative to 7-OH is not established.** Reported mu affinity is
  weaker. Weaker is not safe, and dose compensates, but do not claim equivalence.

### 8. Recommended action

**File a third comment on HHS-OASH-2026-0232 before 2026-09-10.**

The docket is open, the Department asked how the threshold "should apply across
botanical source material, semi-synthetic derivatives, and finished dosage forms,"
and this is a direct, narrow, checkable answer to that question.

**Ask the Department to state whether it considered alkaloids outside the
mitragynine scaffold, and on what basis 9-hydroxycorynantheidine was excluded when
Florida included it.**

**[I] Getting this into the federal record before the product exists is the single
most preventive thing available in this case.**
