### 3. They describe Feel Free without naming it

> "So-called 'herbal supplements' are sold at many gas stations and retailers
> across the country. **Their ingredients include kava extract**, which the company
> says promotes relaxation and lifts mood. **They also include kratom leaf**, which
> is said to enhance energy and focus. **The supplement only contains trace amounts
> of 7-OH, so it would not be regulated under the CSA if 7-OH is scheduled.**"

Kava extract, plus leaf kratom, plus trace 7-OH, sold in gas stations, is
**Feel Free**.

**Their footnote for that sentence is a Botanic Tonics press release**, headlined
that Feel Free "applauds Florida ban" on synthetic 7-OH, dated 2025-08-13. Their
footnote 4 is CNN's coverage of the same product.

**[I] Eleven senators cited the company's own press release celebrating a ban on
its competitor as the evidence that scheduling 7-OH alone protects the wrong
product.** That is this entire case in a footnote, and it is already in the
congressional record.
