# SEVENFOLD / FINDING 23
## The founder of the company on line 25 gave the Republican National Committee the exact legal maximum

**Status:** CONFIRMED from FEC primaries with filing image numbers.
**Analyst:** Aegis, for Obsidian Watch Group. **Date:** 2026-08-28.

---

### 1. Why this finding exists

Finding 01 establishes that Markwayne Mullin holds $500,001-$1,000,000 of Botanic
Tonics LLC directly, that the holding appears on his nominee OGE Form 278e filed
2026-03-17, and that it was left off a divestiture agreement which captured 63
equities including Philip Morris International.

This finding asks the reverse question. Not what the official holds, but what the
company's founder gives.

### 2. The record [P]

FEC Schedule A, queried by `contributor_employer = BOTANIC TONICS`. Employer
fields are self-reported, so these figures are a **floor**. Thirty-seven itemized
rows returned. Seven distinct contributors. One of them is 99.9% of the money.

**Jerry W. Ross, listed occupation "FOUNDER", of Malibu, California: $730,673.**

| Recipient | Amount | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| Republican National Committee | **$443,000** | 2026-02-17 |
| Kennedy Victory Fund 2024 | $161,800 | 2025-01-02, 2025-02-05 |
| Libertarian National Committee, Inc. | $41,300 | 2025-01-21 |
| Eleven state Libertarian / Free New Mexico parties | $77,973 | 2025-01-02 to 2025-02-18 |
| Team Kennedy | $6,600 | 2023-09-19 |

The remaining six Botanic Tonics contributors gave **$820 combined**, in amounts
between $2 and $312. There is no employee giving program here. There is one man.

### 3. The RNC contribution is the statutory ceiling, to the dollar

FEC's published limits for the 2025-2026 cycle, individual donor to a national
party committee [P, fec.gov contribution-limits table]:

| Account | Annual limit |
|---|---|
| National party committee, main | $44,300 |
| Additional national party account (convention) | $132,900 |
| Additional national party account (headquarters) | $132,900 |
| Additional national party account (recount / legal) | $132,900 |
| **Maximum reachable in one year** | **$443,000** |

Ross gave $443,000 on 2026-02-17, in four transactions of $132,900, $132,900,
$132,900 and $44,300. Recipient committee C00003418. FEC image numbers
`202603209853749439`, `202603209853749438`, `202603209853743128`.

That is not approximately the maximum. It is the maximum, exactly, across all four
accounts, on a single day.

**One qualification we make ourselves.** All four rows carry the memo
`PARTNERSHIP ATTRIBUTION`, and three are booked on the line "Other Federal
Receipts (Dividends, Interest, etc.)" rather than the individual contribution
line. That means the funds reached the RNC through a partnership or LLC and were
attributed to Ross as a partner. Whether the money originated in his personal
accounts is **not established by this record** [I]. The attribution to him is the
filing's own.

### 4. Sequence

Dates only. We draw no causal line and none should be read in.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2023-09-19 | Ross gives $6,600 to Team Kennedy, RFK Jr.'s presidential campaign |
| 2025-01-02 | $146,300 to Kennedy Victory Fund 2024 plus ~$46,000 across seven state Libertarian parties, same day |
| 2025-01-21 | $41,300 to the Libertarian National Committee |
| 2025-02-05, 02-18 | second tranche, ~$25,000 |
| 2025 | RFK Jr. confirmed as Secretary of Health and Human Services |
| 2026-02-17 | **$443,000 to the RNC, the exact annual maximum** |
| 2026-03-17 | Mullin's nominee 278 discloses the Botanic Tonics holding |
| 2026-03-18 | Eleven senators write Commissioner Makary urging action on 7-OH |

The joint-fundraising structure is worth naming plainly. A single contribution to
a joint fundraising committee such as Kennedy Victory Fund 2024 is allocated
outward to its participant committees, which is why one donor appears in the
records of eleven state Libertarian parties on the same day. It is a lawful
mechanism for moving a large sum past any single committee's limit. Ross used it,
and then a year later used the RNC's four-account structure the same way.

### 5. Both sides of the fight funded the same candidate

MIT45 employees gave $9,900 to Team Kennedy (Finding 22). Ross gave $168,400
across Kennedy committees. Two kratom companies on opposing sides of the 7-OH
question funded the same presidential candidate, who became the cabinet secretary
under whom the 7-OH action was announced.

### 6. What we assert, and what we do not

**We assert:** the amounts, the dates, the recipients, the exactness of the RNC
ceiling, and that the founder of the company on Mullin's line 25 is the largest
identified political donor in this industry by an order of magnitude.

**We do not assert** that any contribution purchased any official act. We have no
evidence of that, we have not looked for a quid pro quo, and nothing here should
be read as alleging one. Large lawful giving by a company founder is common. We
report it because the same company appears on a sitting official's disclosure,
and the public is entitled to see both facts in one place.
