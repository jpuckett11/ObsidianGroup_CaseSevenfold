# SEVENFOLD / FINDING 24
## A kratom company put $1,000,000 into the Health Secretary's political vehicle, and that vehicle's largest outflow is to a 501(c)(4)

**Status:** CONFIRMED from FEC and IRS primaries.
**Analyst:** Aegis, for Obsidian Watch Group. **Date:** 2026-08-28.

---

### 1. What MAHA PAC actually is [P]

FEC committee **C00821439**. Hybrid PAC with a non-contribution account.
Treasurer **Tony Lyons**. Registered in DC, first filed 2022-07-26.

Its name has changed twice, and the history is the finding:

| Cycle | Registered name |
|---|---|
| 2022 | **PEOPLE'S PHARMA MOVEMENT** |
| 2024 | **AMERICAN VALUES 2024** |
| 2026 | **MAHA PAC** |

American Values 2024 was the principal outside-spending vehicle supporting Robert
F. Kennedy Jr.'s 2024 presidential campaign. Its donor list confirms the identity
beyond argument, on complete per-donor queries rather than a top-N slice:
**Timothy Mellon $25,003,300** across six contributions, **Gavin de Becker
$14,000,000** across four, **Nicole Shanahan $4,000,000** in one.

The same committee, renamed, is now the MAHA movement's PAC, under a Secretary of
Health and Human Services.

### 2. Botanic Tonics is in that donor list [P]

| Date | Amount | Contributor as filed |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-03-04 | $500,000 | BOTANIC TONICS LLC |
| 2026-04-09 | $500,000 | BOTANIC TONICS LLC |

**$1,000,000.** Thirteenth largest itemized receipt out of 834.

Two features of that entry are worth stating precisely. First, it is filed as a
contribution from the **LLC itself**, not from Ross personally, which is lawful
into a hybrid PAC's independent-expenditure account and is a different act from
the personal giving in Finding 23. Second, in a top-donor list composed almost
entirely of **named individuals**, a corporate donor stands out. Botanic Tonics
and Planeta Management LLC are the only two in the top fifteen.

Combined with Ross's $730,673 personal total, the traceable federal political
money attached to this one company is **$1,730,673**.

### 3. Where the money goes after it arrives [P]

MAHA PAC's single largest recurring disbursement is not advertising. It is
transfers to a nonprofit:

**$4,048,428 to MAHA ACTION, INC.**, across six transactions, 2025-2026. Five are
described in the filings as `CHARITABLE CONTRIBUTION`.

| Year | Transferred |
|---|---|
| 2025 | $3,118,415 |
| 2026 | $930,013 |

MAHA Action, Inc. is **EIN 994785075**, Vienna, Virginia, IRS subsection **4**: a
**501(c)(4) social welfare organization** [P, IRS BMF via ProPublica Nonprofit
Explorer]. Verified as the correct entity; the only other search match is an
unrelated North Carolina charity.

A 501(c)(4) does not disclose its donors. Money entering it from a PAC is the
point at which itemized federal traceability ends.

### 4. What we do not claim, stated before anyone has to ask

**We cannot trace Botanic Tonics' dollars into the c4, and we do not assert that
we can.** The chronology forbids it. Every observed MAHA PAC transfer to MAHA
Action is dated **on or before 2026-02-02**. Botanic Tonics' first contribution is
**2026-03-04**, a month later. No transfer we can see post-dates their money.

Money in a PAC is fungible and later filings may show later transfers. But on the
present record the honest statement is this: the pipeline exists and is
documented, Botanic Tonics is documented inside the PAC, and the two facts have
not been connected to each other by any evidence we hold.

**We also do not assert** that any contribution purchased any official act.

### 6. Why this belongs in the case

Finding 01 is about what an official holds. Finding 23 is about what a founder
gives. This finding is about the vehicle that connects the product category to
the department regulating it, and about the specific mechanism by which the
public's ability to follow the money is designed to end.

The company whose product is left on the shelf by the 7-OH action is a
million-dollar donor to the political organization of the cabinet department that
took it. That is not an allegation. It is two federal filings, and they are both
public.
