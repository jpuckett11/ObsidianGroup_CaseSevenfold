# SEVENFOLD / FINDING 30
## Everything this case identified as uncovered can be bought over a counter tomorrow, and still can the day the rule takes effect

**Status:** CONFIRMED by reading the scheduling instruments against the compounds.
**Analyst:** Aegis, for Obsidian Watch Group. **Date:** 2026-08-28.

---

### 1. Why this finding exists

Comments `mtc-q9zv-dase`, `mtd-holx-p7i5` and `mtd-izm8-ql4p` argue that scope
drawn around named molecules cannot hold. That is an argument about regulatory
design, and arguments about regulatory design are easy to file away.

**This finding states the same thing as a fact about a shelf.**

### 2. What the instruments actually cover [P]

| Instrument | What it reaches |
|---|---|
| DEA proposed rule, 2026-07-06 (DEA-2026-1190) | 7-hydroxymitragynine above a threshold |
| DEA temporary order, effective 2026-08-26 | mitragynine pseudoindoxyl, MGM-15, MGM-16 |
| HHS letter, 2026-03-06 | 7-OH alone |

**Four substances. All four on the mitragynine scaffold.**

### 3. What they do not reach

| Compound | Established in | Federal status |
|---|---|---|
| **9-hydroxycorynantheidine** | Findings 20, 27 | **Uncovered.** A **major circulating human metabolite** of mitragynine |
| **Corynoxine** | Finding 28 | **Uncovered.** A **mu-opioid receptor FULL agonist** |
| **Corynoxine B** | Finding 29 | **Uncovered.** Retail products are documented as being **built around it specifically** |
| Corynantheidine | Finding 28 | Uncovered. Mu-opioid partial agonist |
| Rhynchophylline, isorhynchophylline, mitraphylline, corynoxeine, isopteropodine | Finding 28 | Uncovered |

**No purchase is required to establish this.** It is the rule text read against the
compound list. Anyone can check it in an afternoon.

### 4. The part that should be uncomfortable

**Nothing above changes when the threshold takes effect.**

The rule does not phase these in. It does not reach them later. There is no
mechanism in any of the three instruments by which a compound on the corynantheidine
or oxindole side becomes covered. On the day 7-OH is controlled, **a mu-opioid full
agonist and a major human metabolite of the controlled substance remain lawful to
manufacture, market and sell at federal level.**

Jay Puckett who documented this gap can walk into a smoke shop and buy the
substitute over a counter, lawfully, while the rule restricting the original is
still being written. **That is not a hypothetical about future substitution. It is
the present condition.**

### 5. And the substitution is not waiting for permission

Finding 29 established that this is already happening:

- Products marketed as "Cat's Claw" or "Rhynchophylline" **built around corynoxine
  B**, described by the people using them as dodging the 7-OH scheduling.
- Utah Poison Control, 22 July 2026, reporting **multiple severe overdoses** from
  smoke-shop products under those names.
- A **death** in Utah County, 30 July 2026, from mislabelled Buzzers-brand Cat's
  Claw tablets containing MGM-15.

**Two of those three involve compounds the rule does cover**, sold under a name it
does not. The third involves a compound it never reached at all.

### 6. State law is doing what federal law is not

Utah banned kratom products other than pure-leaf in its 2026 legislative session.
Florida's June 2026 emergency rule names 9-hydroxycorynantheidine directly
(Finding 20).

**Legality therefore depends on which state a person is standing in**, and the
federal instrument is the more permissive of the two layers. A federal rule that is
narrower than a state emergency rule issued months earlier is worth the Department
explaining.

### 7. What this finding does not claim

- **It does not allege that any specific retailer is breaking the law.** Selling an
  uncovered compound is lawful, which is the entire point.
- **It does not identify products to purchase**, and deliberately names no vendor
  beyond those already named in a state health warning.
- **It is not a recommendation to schedule these compounds.** This case has said on
  the docket, four times, that molecule-by-molecule scheduling is the failure mode
  and not the fix.
- **OWG has assayed nothing.** No product has been laboratory-tested by us, and the
  compound content of any specific retail item remains unestablished (`LEADS.md`,
  L-01).

### 8. What it is for

The five comments on HHS-OASH-2026-0232 ask the Department whether scope was drawn
by scaffold, by metabolic origin, or by quantity per article.

**This finding is the answer to what the choice costs while it goes unanswered.**
Every day the question sits open, the compounds are on shelves, they are legal, and
the substitution the Department was warned about proceeds without breaking a single
law.
