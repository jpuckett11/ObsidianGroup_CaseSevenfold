# SEVENFOLD / FINDING 32
## Eleven senators wrote 537 words about this substance. None of them were about treating anyone.

**Status:** CONFIRMED by word count on the public letter, `exhibits/ricketts_letter_fda_20260318.pdf`.
**Analyst:** Aegis, for Obsidian Watch Group. **Date:** 2026-08-29.
**Anyone can reproduce this finding in five minutes with the PDF and a search box.**

---

### 1. The letter

On **18 March 2026**, eleven United States Senators wrote to FDA Commissioner
Makary about 7-hydroxymitragynine. **537 words.**

### 2. What is in it

| Category | Occurrences |
|---|---|
| **Enforcement and scheduling** | **12** |
| schedul- | 4 |
| control | 3 |
| ban | 2 |
| enforce | 2 |
| seiz- | 1 |
| **Treatment, recovery, or access to care** | **0** |

**Zero.** Not treatment, not recovery, not buprenorphine, not methadone, not
naloxone, not rehabilitation, not prescriber, not coverage, not insurance.

A single string match on "clinic" resolves to **`mayoclinic.org` in a footnote
URL.** It is not a reference to treatment. **The true count is zero.**

### 3. The one thing they asked for beyond the existing action

> "However, **we encourage you to consider scheduling the whole kratom leaf**, given
> that some of the most accessible and addictive products contain whole kratom leaf,
> not 7-OH."

**They were willing to expand the scope of the letter. They expanded it toward more
scheduling.**

That is the finding in a sentence. Given an open field and a Commissioner's
attention, eleven senators used it to ask for a wider ban and nothing else.

### 4. The fair objection, stated before anyone else makes it

**A letter has a purpose, and this one was about a scheduling decision at FDA.**
Treatment funding is largely appropriations and CMS, not the Commissioner's desk. A
reader could reasonably say the letter is silent on treatment because treatment was
not the subject.

**We do not think that survives their own sentence.** They did not confine
themselves to the pending action. They asked the Commissioner to **consider
scheduling an entire botanical species**, which is a substantial policy expansion.
The letter's scope was elastic. It stretched in one direction only.

### 5. This is not a corruption finding, and that is what makes it worse

**Finding 22 establishes, from FEC primaries with controls, that not one of the
eleven signatories appears as a recipient of money from any kratom company or trade
association.** Not Ricketts, not Chairman Crapo, not Senator Blackburn. We searched
and we published the clean result.

**So this is not a story about bought representatives.** It is a record of what
eleven **unbought** legislators chose to write about, when the subject was a
substance dependency, and 42.4 million Americans with an untreated substance use
disorder were in the federal government's own survey the whole time.

**They were free to write anything. They wrote about enforcement.**

### 6. The asymmetry of speed

| | |
|---|---|
| FDA recommendation to DEA temporary order on 7-OH | roughly **13 months** |
| Senators' letter urging faster action | 2026-03-18 |
| Share of people with a substance use disorder receiving no treatment | **87.6%** |
| Federal action on that gap, at comparable urgency | **none identified in this case** |

**[I] We do not claim Congress has done nothing on treatment.** It has, over years,
and this case has not surveyed that record. **What we document is proportion and
speed.** The molecule moved in about a year, with a senatorial letter pushing it
along. The treatment gap has not moved, and no comparable letter about it appears in
this file.

### 7. And the channel back is closed

`CONTACT_LOG.md` records what happened when a constituent brought documented work to
one of those eleven offices:

- **Two messages submitted** through the web form. **Confirmation page, no tracking
  number of any kind**, so a citizen cannot later prove what they sent or reference
  it.
- **A phone call to the state office**, directed back to that same form, with no
  substantive engagement and no connection to a policy staffer.
- **An email to the conventional Senate address** returned **`550 5.1.1 User
  Unknown`** from the Senate's own mail server. **No senator publishes an email
  address**; the official Senate directory lists none, chamber-wide.

**Recorded without complaint.** Routing callers to a form is ordinary practice and
implies nothing about any individual. **But the structural fact stands: the people
elected to be a voice have built one channel inward, it issues no receipt, and the
published phone number exists to point you back at it.**

### 8. What this finding claims, precisely

- **That the letter contains zero treatment references.** Countable, verifiable.
- **That its only expansion was toward broader scheduling.** Quoted verbatim.
- **That the signatories took no industry money.** Established in Finding 22.
- **That a constituent channel exists which produces no receipt.** Documented in
  `CONTACT_LOG.md`.

**It does not claim** that any senator acted in bad faith, that any of them is
indifferent to addiction, or that scheduling 7-OH is wrong. **This case has said
four times on the federal record that the harm is real.**

What it claims is narrower and harder to answer: **on the single occasion these
eleven officials put this subject in writing, the people already suffering from it
did not appear in the document.**
