# SEVENFOLD / FINDING 39
## West Virginia's own registry, dated six weeks after the federal scheduling action, lists thirty 7-OH products by name and dose. The largest is 210 mg.

**Status:** CONFIRMED **[P]** from the state agency's own published list, obtained
and hashed.
**Analyst:** Aegis, for Obsidian Watch Group. **Date:** 2026-08-29.
**This is the strongest documentary support yet for Finding 30**, and it converts
Finding 38 from an adverse party's assertion into a corroborated fact.

---

## 1. The document [P]

**West Virginia Department of Agriculture, "Registered Kratom Products v
08/14/2026."** Published at `agriculture.wv.gov/registered-kratom-products/`,
retrieved 2026-08-29, 14 pages, **450 numbered product rows.**

Under W. Va. Code § 19-12F, kratom manufacturers, distributors and retailers must
register with the Department, and **every kratom product distributed in the state
must be registered annually**, at $300 a year plus **$200 per product**.

**The version date is 14 August 2026.** The DEA's scheduling action for
7-hydroxymitragynine issued **1 July 2026**. This list postdates it by six weeks.

## 3. Set that against the federal threshold

**Finding 18 established that the federal action carries an absolute limit of 1.00 mg
per article**, alongside the 0.05% by dry weight figure that was reported everywhere.

**A 210 mg tablet, if the label figure refers to 7-OH content, is two hundred and ten
times that limit**, registered with a state government six weeks after the federal
action.

**We state the conditional deliberately.** See §5.

## 4. This corroborates Finding 38 [P]

Finding 38 recorded Botanic Tonics' allegation, in a sworn federal complaint against
West Virginia's Agriculture Commissioner, that the Department had registered
7-OH products including **"Roxy Complex Alkaloids Tablets 16 mg"** and **"20 mg"**
while refusing to register *feel free*. That was labeled `[S, adverse party]` and
flagged for checking against the registry.

**It checks out.** The registry contains six **Roxy Complex Alkaloid Tablets** rows,
registrant **Lifted Made**, at 16 mg and 20 mg, in Lemonade, Mint, Triple Berry,
Strawmelon, Blue Raspberry and Tropical.

**And *feel free* does not appear anywhere in the 450 rows.** Zero hits. The
company's account of being excluded while competitors were registered is, on the
state's own document, accurate.

**The "Roxy Instamix" products BT listed at 128 mg do not appear either.** They may
have lapsed, been withdrawn, or never been registered. We do not know which.

## 5. What we are NOT claiming, and it matters

- **A milligram figure in a product name is a manufacturer's label claim, not an
  assay.** Nothing here is a laboratory result. Whether a "210 mg" tablet contains
  210 mg of 7-hydroxymitragynine is exactly the sort of thing this case has already
  seen labels get wrong: Finding 29 rests on a state alerting the public that labels
  "do not include these drugs as ingredients."
- **"Registered" is not "approved," "tested," or "endorsed."** West Virginia's scheme
  is a registration and fee regime. Registration means a manufacturer filed and paid.
  The Finding 38 complaint alleges the Department demanded no safety proof from any of
  these applicants, and that allegation remains unverified.
- **We do not know these products are still on sale**, only that they are on a list
  dated 14 August 2026.
- **We have not established any relationship between the federal scheduling action and
  this registry.** State registration and federal scheduling are separate regimes and a
  federally scheduled substance can remain on a state list through nothing more than
  administrative lag.

**That last point is the honest one and it cuts against the sharpest reading.** The
existence of this list six weeks after scheduling is not proof of defiance. It may be
proof of a filing cabinet.

## 6. What is nonetheless established, and is not small

**A state government published a document on 14 August 2026 which names thirty
products by the compound the federal government scheduled on 1 July 2026, states
their doses, and identifies who sells them.**

Finding 30 said these products are still on the shelf. **This is the paperwork.**
