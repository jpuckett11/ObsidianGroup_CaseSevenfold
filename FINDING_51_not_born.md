# SEVENFOLD / FINDING 51
## "I was not born an addict. My childhood made me one." The attributable risk fraction is 64 percent, and the genetic framing is the more dangerous claim.

**Status:** The epidemiology is **CONFIRMED [P]** from the primary literature. The
argument in sections 5 and 6 is **[I]** and labeled throughout.
**Analyst:** Aegis, for Obsidian Watch Group. **Date:** 2026-08-29.
**Opened at the investigator's direction, on a statement made by N-01.**

---

## 1. The statement

N-01 said, in substance: **he was not born an addict; his childhood made him one.**

The investigator asked whether the data supports it, and observed that the answer
matters a great deal once genetics enters the conversation, and that the genetic
framing is dangerous.

**Both halves of that are correct. This finding sets out why, and it is not close.**

## 2. What the data says [P]

**Dube, Felitti, Dong, Chapman, Giles and Anda (2003), *Pediatrics* 111(3):564,
DOI `10.1542/peds.111.3.564`.** Retrospective cohort, **8,613 adults**, ten categories
of adverse childhood experience. Verbatim from the abstract:

> "**Each ACE increased the likelihood for early initiation 2- to 4-fold.**"

> "Compared with people with 0 ACEs, **people with ≥5 ACEs were 7- to 10-fold more
> likely** to report illicit drug use problems, **addiction to illicit drugs**, and
> parenteral drug use."

> "**The attributable risk fractions as a result of ACEs** for each of these illicit
> drug use problems were **56%, 64%, and 67%**, respectively."

> "**ACEs seem to account for one half to two thirds of serious problems with drug
> use.**"

**The attributable risk fraction for addiction to illicit drugs is 64 percent.**

That is the technical form of what N-01 said. **Roughly two thirds of serious drug
addiction in that cohort is attributable to what happened to people before they were
old enough to consent to any of it.**

## 3. The finding inside the finding, which is the strongest part [P]

The same paper reports something that matters more than the odds ratios:

> "The persistent graded relationship between the ACE score and initiation of drug use
> **for 4 successive birth cohorts dating back to 1900** suggests that the effects of
> adverse childhood experiences **transcend secular changes such as increased
> availability of drugs, social attitudes toward drugs, and recent massive expenditures
> and public information campaigns to prevent drug use.**"

**Read that against this entire case file.**

Four birth cohorts, back to 1900. Across Prohibition, the post-war boom, the crack
years, the OxyContin years, and now this one. **Across every change in what was for
sale, what it was called, what it cost, and what the government said about it.**

**The relationship between childhood adversity and addiction did not move.**

Findings 34, 42, 44, 48 and 50 document a century's worth of the other approach:
schedule the molecule, police the person, fund the enforcement. **The paper says, from
1900 to the present, that this is not the variable that moves the outcome.**

## 4. What the genetics actually shows, stated fairly [P/S]

**Nobody serious disputes that substance use disorders are heritable.** Twin and
adoption studies have found substantial heritability for decades, commonly reported
around half of population variance. **This finding does not dispute it and would be
worthless if it did.** `[S]` We have not independently verified a specific heritability
point estimate and are not quoting one.

**What the molecular genetics shows is the part that gets left out.** The largest
studies in the field needed **1.2 million individuals** (Liu et al., *Nature Genetics*
2019, DOI `10.1038/s41588-018-0307-5`) and then **3.4 million** (Saunders et al.,
*Nature* 2022, DOI `10.1038/s41586-022-05477-4`) to resolve the architecture of tobacco
and alcohol use, finding hundreds of loci of individually tiny effect.

**[I]** That is what a highly polygenic trait looks like. **It tells you a great deal
about populations and very little about a person.** A cohort of that size is required
precisely because no individual variant, and no practical combination of them, predicts
who becomes dependent.

## 5. [I] Why the two numbers are not in competition, and why people think they are

**This is the conceptual error the investigator is pointing at, and it has a shape.**

**Heritability is a ratio of variances within a given population and a given range of
environments. It is not a measure of how much of a condition is "caused by genes," and
it says nothing at all about how changeable the condition is.**

The standard illustration is height. Height is among the most heritable human traits
measured. **Average height in many countries rose several inches in a century**, on
nutrition alone. **High heritability and high malleability sat in the same trait at the
same time**, and nobody concluded from the twin studies that feeding children was
pointless.

**Attributable risk fraction answers a different question.** It asks: if this exposure
were removed, how much of the outcome would go with it? **Dube's answer for addiction
to illicit drugs is 64 percent.**

**A trait can be substantially heritable and substantially attributable to childhood
adversity at the same time**, because genes very often act **through** environment:
altering how hard an event lands, and altering which environments a person ends up in.

**So N-01's statement and the heritability literature are not in conflict. He is not
denying he had a loaded gun. He is saying something pulled the trigger, and he is
right about what.**

## 6. [I] Why the genetic framing is dangerous, specifically

The investigator called it dangerous. **It is, and the danger is not abstract. It is
four specific moves.**

1. **It relocates the defect into the person.** "Born this way" sounds compassionate
   and lands as a diagnosis of the individual rather than of what was done to them.
2. **It implies immutability, which the science does not support.** See section 5.
   High heritability has never meant unchangeable in any other trait and does not here.
3. **It licenses disinvestment.** If the cause is in the genome, then treatment is
   management rather than repair, prevention is impossible, and a legislature declining
   to fund either is being realistic rather than negligent. **Findings 43, 48, 49 and
   50 document exactly that posture with dollar figures attached.**
4. **It moves the money to control.** A condition understood as constitutional gets
   policed. A condition understood as caused gets prevented. **Finding 50: every dollar
   any American state has appropriated on account of kratom went to enforcement.**

**The 64 percent figure is dangerous to the opposite party.** It means most of this was
preventable, that the prevention window was childhood, and that the failure was not
biological.

## 7. What this changes in the case file

**`REMEDIATION.md` should carry this and currently does not.** Its argument is that
people need a trade and a purpose, and it rests largely on contingency management and
the European rehabilitation literature. **It is missing the upstream half.**

**[I] The revised claim:** if roughly two thirds of serious addiction traces to
childhood adversity, then the remediation argument has **two** limbs, not one.
Downstream, give the adult a trade and the means to stay. **Upstream, the intervention
is the child**, and every year that is not done reloads the number.

**And it sharpens Finding 45.** That finding priced the return on treating the existing
untreated population. **It did not price prevention at all**, and on this evidence
prevention is where the larger number is.

## 8. What is NOT established

- **ACE studies are retrospective and self-reported.** Recall bias is the standard and
  correct criticism, and Dube 2003 carries it. The 1900-cohort persistence is a partial
  answer, not a complete one.
- **Attributable risk fraction assumes the association is causal.** ACEs and addiction
  share confounders, including parental substance use, which is both an ACE category
  and a genetic transmission route. **This is the sharpest objection and it is real.**
- **We have not verified a heritability point estimate** and deliberately quote none.
- **N-01's own history is his, not evidence.** This finding tests a general claim
  against population data. **It does not analyze him and must never be read as doing
  so.**
- **No claim is made that genetics is unimportant**, only that heritability does not
  mean fixed and does not justify not acting.
