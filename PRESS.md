## Three stories, in order of how fast you can verify them

### 1. A company advertised the loophole. Then someone died.

A brand called **Buzzers** states on its own homepage that it "figured out a way to
**extract an indole alkaloid from the Cat's Claw plant to mirror the effects of
popular kratom products**," producing "a **non-kratom product with kratom-like
effects**." A retailer states the reason plainly: cat's claw "**is not a controlled
substance, so Buzzers ship nationwide**."

It is marketed as an **energy and focus supplement**.

On **22 July 2026** the Utah Poison Control Center named Buzzers and Homiez in an
alert warning of "**life-threatening opioid overdose, dependence, or withdrawal**,"
reporting **multiple severe overdoses**, and stating the labels "**do not include
these drugs as ingredients**." On **30 July** a **death in Utah County** was linked
to mislabelled Buzzers-brand tablets containing **MGM-15**.

**Verify in 10 minutes:** the pages are live, and our captures are hashed and dated
at `exhibits/marketing/buzzers/`. The Utah alert is public. Finding 31.

### 2. Eleven senators wrote 537 words about this. None were about treatment.

On **18 March 2026** eleven US Senators wrote to the FDA Commissioner about 7-OH.

| | |
|---|---|
| References to enforcement and scheduling | **12** |
| References to treatment, recovery or care | **0** |

The only "clinic" match is `mayoclinic.org` in a footnote URL. Their sole expansion
beyond the pending action was asking the Commissioner to consider "**scheduling the
whole kratom leaf**."

**None of the eleven took a dollar from this industry** — we checked FEC records and
published the clean result. **This is not a corruption story.** It is a record of
what unbought legislators chose to write about.

**Verify in 5 minutes:** download the letter, search for "treatment." Finding 32.

### 3. The founder gave the RNC the exact legal maximum, on one day.

**Jerry W. Ross**, founder of **Botanic Tonics**, gave the Republican National
Committee **$443,000 on 2026-02-17** — which is precisely the annual statutory
ceiling across all four permitted party accounts ($44,300 + 3 × $132,900).

The company separately put **$1,000,000** into **MAHA PAC**, formerly *American
Values 2024*, the RFK Jr. presidential vehicle. That committee's largest recurring

**All of it lawful.** That is the finding, not an accusation. Findings 23 and 24.
