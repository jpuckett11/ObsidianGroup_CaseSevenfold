## 3. DO NOT SAY. These will be corrected on you.

- **Do not say the government dropped the case *because* of anything.** We have a
  sequence and a twelve-day gap. **We have no evidence of instruction and do not allege
  it.**
- **Do not say the Utah litigation caused, or delayed, the July poison control alert or
  the Utah County death.** F37 says twice that no such claim is made.
- **Do not say Tennessee deleted kratom from a treatment statute.** **We said that and
  it was wrong.** It is a **conforming amendment** to a recovery-residence certification
  standard; once possession became a crime, naming kratom in a list of *nonillicit*
  drugs was simply incorrect. **Substantive effect is most likely nil.** (F44 §5)
- **Do not say four states are running a kratom playbook.** **We said that and it was
  wrong.** Two states on kratom; the other two are hemp cases by a different company.
  (F40 §4)
- **Do not say the industry is winning on preemption.** It is winning where states wrote
  **in-state-only** licensing clauses. Utah wrote none and Utah won. (F41)
- **Do not call the 210 mg figure a measured 7-OH quantity.** It is a **label claim**.
  Nobody has assayed one. (F39 §5)
- **Do not say states fund no treatment.** They do, through Medicaid, SOR grants and
  settlement money. **Our ledger is kratom-attributable appropriations only.** (F50 §7)
- **Do not name the individual behind N-01, and do not present his history as
  evidence.** F51 tests a population claim and says explicitly it must never be read as
  analyzing him.
