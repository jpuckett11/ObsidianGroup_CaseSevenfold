# CASE SEVENFOLD / REMEDIATION
## Why this will keep happening, and the only thing that stops it

**This document is argument, not finding.** Everything in the numbered findings is
sourced and checkable. This is what the investigator concludes from them, stated
plainly and labelled as a conclusion so a reader can disagree with it without
disputing the evidence.

---

## 1. The ladder is not a prediction. This case watched it happen.

| Step | What was done | What appeared |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7-OH proposed for Schedule I above a threshold | |
| 2 | Temporary order adds mitragynine pseudoindoxyl, MGM-15, MGM-16 | |
| 3 | | **Corynoxine B products on smoke-shop shelves**, lawful, uncovered (Finding 29) |
| 4 | | Products sold under **a different plant's name**, some spiked with the scheduled compounds. **A death in Utah** |

Each rung took months, not years. **DEA scheduled MGM-16 while stating no evidence
supported its presence on the market**, on the express reasoning that sellers
substitute. That reasoning was correct. It was also applied to one chemical family
and not the next one over, and the next one over is what is being sold today.

## 2. The chemistry is not a list. Scheduling is.

One plant contains **at least 54 alkaloids**. A scaffold has many positions and
each position can be hydroxylated, methylated, demethylated or oxidised.

- Finding 27: **the human body itself produces an uncovered compound** from the
  covered one. You cannot schedule your way past metabolism.
- Finding 25: **two patent families already claim manufacture without a plant**,
  cell-free enzymes and fermentation, priority dates in 2023. No farm, no import,
  no leaf to test at a border.
- Finding 28: the relevant pharmacology **crosses the genus line** into plants sold
  under a different regulatory framework entirely.

A chemist with an undergraduate degree and a supplier can move faster than a
rulemaking. That is not cynicism. It is the observed cycle time in the table above.

**Every molecule scheduled is a molecule the next product will not contain.**

## 3. The demand does not move, so the supply reorganises around it

This is the part that gets left out.

Scheduling a compound does nothing to the person who needs it at 6am to not be
sick. It changes what is on the shelf. It does not change who walks in.

**From this case's own FTC complaint:**

> A purchaser who knows the product is an opioid, knows the serving size, has a
> physician, can afford treatment and has stable housing can extricate himself,
> with difficulty. We have documented one such case: three weeks, 6,000 mg, and a
> prescribed course of buprenorphine.
>
> A purchaser who has been told the product is botanical supplementation, who has
> not seen the habit-forming disclosure because it sits on a different page, who
> has no available appointment and no coverage, and who does not recognise opioid
> withdrawal when it begins, **has one immediately available intervention. It is to
> buy more of the product.**

That is the whole mechanism. As long as that second person exists in numbers,
**there is a market, and a market will be served**, by whatever molecule is
currently legal.

## 4. What actually removes the market

**Treat the person, or keep chasing the molecule forever.**

Access is the binding constraint, and it is not a small gap at the margins. In the
**Veterans Health Administration**, a system explicitly built to deliver this care,
**half of veterans with opioid use disorder do not receive medication for it**
(*Substance Use & Addiction Journal*, 2026, DOI 10.1177/29767342261442456). That is
the best-resourced case in the country. Everyone else does worse.

The investigator got out. What it took:

- a physician who would prescribe
- buprenorphine with naloxone, actually dispensed
- money, stable housing, and time
- enough technical knowledge to read an FDA scientific assessment and understand
  what he had been sold

**Most people have none of those four.** The product does not discriminate. Access
to the exit does. That asymmetry is Finding 19 and it is the centre of this case.

## 5. Addicted to their passions

The investigator's phrase, and it is not sentimentality.

Something occupies the hours. Dependence is a full-time occupation: acquiring,
dosing, managing withdrawal, hiding it. Remove the substance and the hours remain,
and nothing has been put in them.

Medication handles the physiology. **It does not answer what the day is for.** The
people who stay out are, overwhelmingly, the people who found something that ate
the same hours: work that meant something, a craft, a family they had to be present
for, a fight worth having.

**That is not a soft add-on to treatment. It is the retention half of it**, and it
is the half nobody funds, because it does not fit in a billing code and does not
produce a number a department can put in a press release.

Funding it looks like: opening the prescriber pipeline, paying for the months after
the taper and not just the taper, and putting money into the things that give a
person somewhere to be. It is unglamorous, it is slow, and it does not generate a
press conference with a podium and a schedule number.

> "The machine doesn't care about true freedom or the people any more. It cares

**This is his conclusion, not a finding.** It is recorded because he holds it,

What the case does establish, and what a reader can check without agreeing with the

- That company's founder gave the RNC **the exact statutory maximum**, $443,000, in

- The scientific paper FDA relies on was **paid for by a company in the sector** and
  written by its paid consultants, one of whom came from **JUUL** (Finding 06).
- A **$980,000** lobbying vehicle with **no disclosed funder** lobbied DEA and DOJ
  directly (Finding 14).
- Of **27,053 comments** on the open docket, the questions that matter most were
  asked **once**, by a private citizen from Columbia, Tennessee (Finding 26).
- A citizen's own senator's office has **one contact channel, it issues no receipt**,
  and the published phone line routes callers back to it (`CONTACT_LOG.md`).

**We do not allege a conspiracy and this case never has.** Every one of those facts
has an innocent explanation available. What we say is narrower and harder to
dismiss: **the incentives all point one way, and nobody in the structure is
required to notice.**

## 7. What we actually ask for

Not prohibition. Not a plant ban. We have said so on the docket in writing.

1. **Regulate by quantity per article and by product form, not by molecule.** A
   measure indifferent to scaffold reaches the next compound automatically. A list
   never will. And **define "article"** — as written, forty pills at 0.9 mg each are
   each compliant and deliver 36 mg in one bag (Finding 18).
2. **Enforce the labelling law that already exists.** A product sold as
   "botanical supplementation" while the seller's own help page concedes it is
   habit-forming needs no new statute. It needs Section 5.
3. **Fund the exit.** Prescriber capacity, coverage past the taper, and the
   unglamorous months afterward. Every dollar here removes a customer permanently.
   Every dollar spent scheduling one more molecule moves a customer to the next one.
4. **Publish the reasoning.** The full list of alkaloids considered and the
   disposition of each. If scope was drawn around a scaffold rather than around
   metabolism, say so, and the public can argue with it.

## 8. The honest close

**Nothing in this document will happen quickly, and the ladder in Section 1 will
add another rung before it does.**

That is the argument for filing on the record anyway. Comments `mtc-56yj-nmij`,
`mtc-6i56-o6pm`, `mtc-q9zv-dase` and `mtd-holx-p7i5` sit in an administrative
record with tracking numbers on them. They do not require anyone to care. They will
be there when someone finally goes looking for the moment a person said this in
advance.

Somebody in Utah is dead, and the product that killed them wore a different plant's
name on the label. That happened in July. This document exists so that when the
next one is on shelves under a third name, the record shows it was foreseeable, it
was foreseen, and it was written down.
