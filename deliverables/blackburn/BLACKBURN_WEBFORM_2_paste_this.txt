FIRST: THE PATENTS, WITH THE SPECIFICS

I said two companies have claimed how to manufacture 9-hydroxycorynantheidine. Here is what your staff can verify in an afternoon.

Mitradyne Corporation, EP 4 538 367, priority 20 September 2023 from a US provisional, US application 18/891,491. Its claims are the finding:

Claim 5 covers the enzyme that hydroxylates corynantheidine at the C9 position to produce 9-hydroxycorynantheidine.

Claim 6, the very next one, covers the enzyme that hydroxylates mitragynine at the C7 position to produce 7-hydroxymitragynine.

Read those together. The same document, in consecutive claims, covers the enzyme that makes the compound the government is scheduling and the enzyme that makes the one it is not. Whoever drafted it understood both positions on both scaffolds in 2023. Claim 11 specifies the system is cell-free, meaning no plant is required at all.

Claim 8 gives the biosynthetic order and it is the most important line in any of this: corynantheidine, then 9-hydroxycorynantheidine, then mitragynine. The uncovered compound is an intermediate on the route to the covered one. It is not something a manufacturer would have to set out to make. It is a mandatory waypoint that any enzymatic mitragynine facility passes through and can stop at.

The second is CB Therapeutics of Carlsbad, California, US 2025/0179544, priority 1 December 2023, which claims recombinant-cell production, meaning fermentation, of corynanthe-type alkaloids and names the compound in its specification.

Neither company has done anything wrong and I want to be clear about that. Both are legitimate biotechnology filings, neither states any intent to market this compound, most patent applications are never commercialised, and we found no connection between either company and any party or official in this matter. The inventors on the first are four scientists at the University of Guelph whose published work is cell-free synthesis of plant compounds. I cite them only as proof that manufacture at scale is feasible and has already been claimed, which is what makes the scope question urgent instead of theoretical.
