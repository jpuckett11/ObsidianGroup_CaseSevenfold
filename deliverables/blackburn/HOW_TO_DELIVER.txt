DELIVERING THE BLACKBURN LETTER

Aegis cannot submit this one. Her web form is gated by Google reCAPTCHA plus a
CSRF token, and that control exists to confirm a human constituent is writing.
Defeating it would also be the easiest thing for her office to hold against the
filing. These routes need no captcha.

RECOMMENDED: NASHVILLE OFFICE, CALL THEN MAIL

State offices handle constituent casework. DC handles legislation and is buried.
Nashville is about 45 minutes from Columbia.

  Nashville Office
  719 Church Street, Suite 2100
  Nashville, TN 37203
  Phone: (629) 800-6600
  Fax:   (615) 298-2148

Call first. Ask for the staffer who handles health policy or FDA matters. Say you
are a constituent in Columbia, that you have filed three comments on the HHS
docket on 7-OH, and that you have a three-page letter and a documented case file
for the Senator because she signed the March 18 letter to Commissioner Makary.

Then send it. A letter that arrives after a call gets opened by someone expecting
it. A letter that arrives cold gets counted as correspondence.

WHAT TO LEAD WITH ON THE PHONE

Not the money. Not the holding. Lead with this:

  "Every compound the government has scheduled sits on one chemical scaffold.
  There is a second one in the same plant that nothing covers, Florida already
  named it, and two companies have already patented how to manufacture it."

That is thirty seconds, it is checkable, and it is the part that is hers to act on.
