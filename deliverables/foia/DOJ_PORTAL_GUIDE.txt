==============================================================================
DOJ FOIA — SUBMISSION GUIDE
==============================================================================

WHERE

  FOIA STAR portal:
    https://doj-foia.entellitrak.com/etk-doj-foia-prod/login.request.do

  Or via the national portal:
    https://www.foia.gov  ->  find the component  ->  submit

  Account required. Portal issues a tracking number, which is why this route
  beats mail: a request with no number cannot be chased.

WHICH COMPONENT

  Primary:   Executive Office for United States Attorneys (EOUSA)
             EOUSA holds USAO case files, which is what we are after.

  Secondary: Civil Division, if EOUSA refers or denies on custody grounds.

  If the portal makes you pick one, pick EOUSA. If it lets you name more than
  one, name both.

REQUESTER CATEGORY

  Select "news media" or "educational/non-commercial scientific" if offered.
  It changes fee treatment. The fee waiver is requested regardless.

WHAT THIS ASKS, IN ONE LINE

  Why the United States dismissed its own in rem forfeiture action against
  Botanic Tonics in December 2025, after seizing roughly $3 million of product
  in April 2023 at FDA's request.

  Case: United States v. 250,000 Filled Bottles of Liquid Product,
        No. 4:23-cv-00168-GAG-CDL (N.D. Okla.)

WHY IT MATTERS TO THE REST OF THE CASE

  Finding 24 records the sequence without asserting a connection:

    2025-12    DOJ drops the seizure case
    2026-03-04 Botanic Tonics sends MAHA PAC $500,000
    2026-03-17 Mullin nominee 278 discloses the holding
    2026-04-09 Second $500,000
    2026-07-01 DEA moves on 7-OH only

  A dismissal memo would either explain that first line innocently or it would
  not. Either result is worth having, and it is the only item in this case where
  the government must answer on a clock.

EXPECT

  20 working days statutory, routinely exceeded. Expedited processing is
  requested in the letter. If nothing arrives in 30 days, the portal tracking
  number is what lets you follow up.
