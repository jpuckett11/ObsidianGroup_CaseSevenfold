This is a request under the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. Section 552.

BACKGROUND

In April 2023, at the request of the Food and Drug Administration, United States
Marshals seized more than 250,000 units of kratom-containing product and more than
1,000 kilograms of bulk kratom, valued at approximately $3 million, from Botanic
Tonics, LLC in Broken Arrow, Oklahoma. The government commenced the above in rem
action.

On 10 December 2025 the Court DENIED the claimant's motion to dismiss the
government's First Amended Verified Complaint. Dkt. No. 33. The Court held that the
Amended Complaint survives and that the alleged facts "support a reasonable belief
that, if proven, the Government would be able to successfully meet its burden."

On or about 22 December 2025, twelve days later, the government filed a Notice of
Dismissal, dismissing the action without prejudice on its own motion.

The government had litigated this action for two years and seven months. It alleged
that the seized articles were adulterated under 21 U.S.C. 342(f)(1)(B) because kratom
is a new dietary ingredient for which there is inadequate information to assure it
does not present a significant or unreasonable risk of illness or injury, and it
cited addiction and liver toxicity. It opposed the motion to dismiss and prevailed.

This request seeks the records reflecting the decision to abandon that action twelve
days after prevailing on it, including:

1. All internal memoranda, recommendations, decision papers, and approval records
   relating to the decision to dismiss, including any litigation risk assessment
   or settlement authority memorandum.

2. All communications between the Department of Justice and the Food and Drug
   Administration, the Department of Health and Human Services, or the Office of
   the Assistant Secretary for Health concerning this action or its dismissal.

3. All communications between the Department and any person outside the federal
   government concerning this action or its dismissal, including communications
   with counsel for Botanic Tonics, LLC, and with any registered lobbyist. This
   request expressly includes communications with Checkmate Government Relations,
   Miller Strategies LLC, Holland & Knight LLP, Troutman Strategies, and any
   person acting on behalf of Botanic Tonics, LLC, Stop Gas Station Heroin LLC,
   the Global Kratom Coalition, the Kratom Trade Association, or Botanicals for
   Better Health and Wellness.

4. All records reflecting any contact concerning kratom or 7-hydroxymitragynine
   between the Department and the Executive Office of the President, the Office of
   Management and Budget, or the Office of the Secretary of Homeland Security.

5. Calendar entries, meeting notices, visitor logs and call logs reflecting
   meetings or calls concerning this action or concerning kratom or
   7-hydroxymitragynine regulation generally.

6. The disposition record and any final case-closing memorandum.

FEE WAIVER REQUESTED

A fee waiver is requested under 5 U.S.C. Section 552(a)(4)(A)(iii). Disclosure is
in the public interest: it concerns the government's decision to abandon a
multi-million dollar enforcement action against a manufacturer in which a
Cabinet-level official has disclosed a financial interest of $500,001 to
$1,000,000, during a period in which the same product category was the subject of
federal rulemaking. Obsidian Watch Group is an independent investigative
organization. We seek these records for public dissemination at no charge, have no
commercial interest in the outcome, and no financial interest in any company named
in this request.

EXPEDITED PROCESSING REQUESTED

Expedited processing is requested under 5 U.S.C. Section 552(a)(6)(E). There is an
urgency to inform the public: the Department of Health and Human Services has an
open Request for Information on the 7-hydroxymitragynine scheduling threshold
(docket HHS-OASH-2026-0232) with a comment period closing 10 September 2026, and
the Senate Finance Committee has an open inquiry with a 31 August 2026 response
date. Records bearing on the government's enforcement posture toward this product
category are directly relevant to both proceedings and lose public value after
they close.
