This is a request under the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. Section 552.

RECORDS REQUESTED

For the period 1 January 2025 to the present:

1. All records reflecting any addition, removal, alteration, or proposed
   alteration to content on FDA web properties concerning kratom, mitragynine, or
   7-hydroxymitragynine. This includes the page currently at
   fda.gov/news-events/public-health-focus/fda-and-kratom. For each change,
   please provide the prior and revised content, the date, the person or office
   requesting it, and the scientific or policy basis relied upon.

2. All communications between FDA or HHS personnel and any Member of Congress,
   Congressional staff, or official of another federal department concerning
   kratom consumer-facing content on FDA web properties. This request expressly
   includes communications with the office of Senator Markwayne Mullin and with
   the Office of the Secretary of Homeland Security.

3. All records reflecting a decision to decline any requested change to kratom
   content, including any record of a determination to retain the warning
   regarding liver toxicity, seizures, and substance use disorder.

4. Concerning the FDA assessment titled "7-Hydroxymitragynine (7-OH): An
   Assessment of the Scientific Data and Toxicological Concerns Around an
   Emerging Opioid Threat":

   a. Records identifying the source intended by the in-text citation
      "(Kruegel et al., 2019)" appearing in the discussion of 7-OH content by
      weight. No such reference appears in the document's reference list.

   b. Records reflecting any review of funding sources, sponsorship, or author
      conflicts of interest for the literature cited in that assessment,
      including but not limited to Huestis, Brett, Bothmer and Atallah (2024),
      Molecules 29(5), a study disclosed as funded by Johnson Foods LLC and
      authored by that company's paid consultants.

   c. The analytical methodology, sampling basis, and measurement uncertainty
      underlying the proposed 0.05 percent by dry weight threshold.

5. Concerning docket HHS-OASH-2026-0232: all records identifying the party or
   parties who requested the extension of the comment period granted on
   26 August 2026 (91 FR, document 2026-17409), and all communications concerning
   that request.

FEE WAIVER AND EXPEDITED PROCESSING

A fee waiver is requested under 5 U.S.C. Section 552(a)(4)(A)(iii), and expedited
processing under 5 U.S.C. Section 552(a)(6)(E). The Department's own comment period
on the threshold closes 10 September 2026. Records concerning the evidentiary basis
for that threshold, and concerning contemporaneous alterations to consumer safety
information about the product category the rule does not reach, are directly
material to that open proceeding and to a pending Senate Finance Committee inquiry.

Obsidian Watch Group is an independent investigative organization with no
commercial interest in kratom or 7-hydroxymitragynine and no funding from any
party to this proceeding. Records will be disseminated publicly at no charge.
