This is a request under the West Virginia Freedom of Information Act, W. Va. Code
29B-1-1 et seq. It is directed to the Tax Division as the custodian of the records
described, per 29B-1-3(b).

BACKGROUND

W. Va. Code 19-12F-7(a) levies "a privilege tax equal to 11 percent of the retail
sales price of kratom or kratom products sold during the reporting period." Subsection
(f) provides that all money received from that tax "shall be deposited into the
Agricultural Fees Fund created by 19-1-4c of this code." The tax is payable quarterly
to the Tax Commissioner under 19-12F-7(c).

RECORDS REQUESTED

For each fiscal year, or each quarterly reporting period, from the first period in
which the tax was collected through the most recent period for which figures are
available:

1. The total dollar amount of privilege tax collected under W. Va. Code 19-12F-7,
   stated in aggregate.

2. The total dollar amount of that revenue transferred or deposited into the
   Agricultural Fees Fund under 19-12F-7(f).

3. The number of taxpayers filing returns under 19-12F-7, stated as a count only.

4. Any report, summary, dashboard or internal accounting prepared by the Tax Division
   that states aggregate collections under this section, including any submitted to
   the Legislature, the Governor's Office, the Department of Agriculture, or the
   Alcohol Beverage Control Administration.

5. Any memorandum of understanding entered into under W. Va. Code 19-12F-7(h) or
   19-12F-9(d) between the Tax Commissioner, the Commissioner of Agriculture, and the
   Alcohol Beverage Control Administration Commissioner.

WHAT IS NOT REQUESTED, STATED PLAINLY

I am aware of W. Va. Code 11-10-5d and I am not asking the Division to violate it.

I do not seek any return, any part of a return, or any return information. I do not
seek information concerning the personal affairs of any individual or the business of
any single firm or corporation. I do not seek taxpayer names, identification numbers,
addresses, filing histories, or any figure attributable to a particular taxpayer.

Items 1 through 3 are requested as aggregate figures across all filers.

If for any period the number of filers is small enough that an aggregate figure would

I ask that the Division:

   (a) provide the figure at a broader level of aggregation, for example annual rather
       than quarterly, or combined across periods, that resolves the concern; or

   (b) if no level of aggregation resolves it, say so, and state the number of periods
       withheld on that basis and the number of filers involved.

I would rather have a smaller true number than a denial.

WHY THIS IS SOUGHT

Obsidian Watch Group is an independent research organization. It has published a case
file on the federal 7-hydroxymitragynine scheduling action at
github.com/jpuckett11/ObsidianGroup_CaseSevenfold, free and without paywall, including
findings that rest on the West Virginia Department of Agriculture's own published
Registered Kratom Products list.

The specific question is simple and, so far as I can establish, unanswered anywhere in
public: how much money does West Virginia raise from this tax, and therefore how much
is flowing into the Agricultural Fees Fund for administering and enforcing Article 12F.
The statute directs the revenue to a public fund for a public purpose. The size of it
should not be a mystery to the people paying it, particularly since 19-12F-7(b)
forbids retailers from showing the tax as a line item on a receipt.

I hold no commercial interest of any kind in kratom, in any company in this sector, or
in the outcome of any related proceeding.
