1. ReportFraud.ftc.gov     <- the fast one, creates a Consumer Sentinel record
                              that state AGs and other agencies can see

   Path: "Something else" -> "Products and services"
   Paste: FTC_REPORTFRAUD_paste_this.txt   (3,572 chars, sized for the form)

   If the field is shorter than that, keep the first three sections. The two
   quotes from one domain on the same day are the whole complaint; everything
   else supports them.

2. Bureau of Consumer Protection, Division of Advertising Practices
                              <- the substantive one

   Mail: 600 Pennsylvania Avenue NW, Washington DC 20580
   Send: OWG_FTC_Complaint_2026-08-28.pdf  (4 pages, the full complaint)

   Call the Consumer Response Center, 877-382-4357, for the current mailing
   address for the Division, and say it is an industry complaint rather than an
   individual consumer complaint. That routes it differently.

   THERE IS NO EMAIL INTAKE. Checked 2026-08-29. ReportFraud is a JavaScript
   form with no API and the Bureau route is postal. Both need a person.

WHAT MAKES THIS ONE STRONG

Not the harm data. Agencies already have harm data.

It is the two quotes from the same domain on the same day. The sales page says
botanical supplementation; the help centre says habit-forming, addiction and
withdrawal. That is the seller's own knowledge, in their own words, placed where
buyers will not encounter it. Lead with it every time.

It also does not depend on the investigator's own account, on any conflict of
interest finding, or on any rulemaking. It is a labelling case and it stands
alone.
