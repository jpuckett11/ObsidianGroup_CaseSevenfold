FILING THE FTC COMPLAINT

TWO ROUTES. Do both.

1. ReportFraud.ftc.gov
   The public intake. Fast, creates a record in the Consumer Sentinel database
   that state AGs and other agencies can see. Choose "Something else" ->
   "Products and services". Paste the summary and the requested actions. There is
   a character limit, so lead with the botanical-versus-synthetic contradiction
   and the seller's own habit-forming admission.

2. Bureau of Consumer Protection, Division of Advertising Practices
   The substantive route. Email or mail the full complaint as a PDF.
   Mail: 600 Pennsylvania Avenue NW, Washington DC 20580
   Ask the Consumer Response Center (877-382-4357) for the current address for
   the Division of Advertising Practices and reference an industry complaint
   rather than an individual consumer complaint.

WHAT MAKES THIS ONE STRONG

Not the harm data. Agencies already have harm data.

It is the two quotes from the same domain on the same day. The sales page says
botanical supplementation; the help centre says habit-forming, addiction, and
withdrawal. That is the seller's own knowledge, in their own words, placed where
buyers will not encounter it. Lead with it every time.

TIME SENSITIVE

Product pages change. Three of nine target pages already refused capture. The
"all natural" and "botanical" language will very likely be revised once
scheduling takes effect. Everything captured 2026-08-27 is hashed in
exhibits/marketing/MANIFEST.tsv. Re-capture periodically and preserve the
differences; a page that quietly drops the word "botanical" after a complaint is
itself evidence.

STATE ROUTE

Tennessee Attorney General, Consumer Protection Division, is the natural second
filing. Georgia's AG has already issued a public alert calling these synthetic
opioids, which makes the framing familiar rather than novel. The same document
works with the addressee changed.
