This form is not medical advice and we are not clinicians. If you are trying to
stop, talk to a doctor first. Opioid withdrawal is usually not life threatening
on its own, but dehydration and other conditions can make it dangerous, and there
are medications that make it easier.

**Do not take or obtain any product in order to answer this.** We only want to
hear from people who already have this experience.

---

## What this is

We are documenting what actually happens to people who use 7-OH
(7-hydroxymitragynine) products. These are the concentrated tablets, shots,
gummies and drink mixes sold in gas stations and smoke shops.

The federal government is deciding right now how to regulate these products. In
its own scientific assessment, the FDA admits: *"a well-designed human abuse
potential study has not been conducted."* There is a public comment docket open
until **10 September 2026**, and the government has asked for input.

So the people who have actually used these products are, right now, the best
available evidence on what they do. That is why we are asking.

## Your consent

Please read this. Ask us anything before you fill in the form.

1. **This is voluntary.** You can skip any question and stop at any point.
2. **You choose how your account is used.** Section H below. The default is that
   nothing is shared outside our files unless you say otherwise.
3. **If you allow public use, we remove identifying details** — your name,
   contact details, exact town, employer, and anything else that could identify
   you — unless you specifically tell us to keep something.
4. **You can withdraw.** Contact us and we remove your account from our files.
   Once something has been filed with a federal agency it becomes part of a
   public record we cannot retract, so tell us before that step if you are unsure.
5. **We are not the government and not police.** We are an independent
   investigative group. We are not asking for anything that identifies where you
   bought anything or who sold it to you, and you should not tell us.
6. **We will not pay you** and we are not selling anything.
7. **Legal note.** 7-OH is not currently a federally scheduled substance. Some
   states and localities have restricted it. We do not ask about anything illegal
   and you should not describe anything that exposes you legally.
