# Aggregating accounts for submission
### Template · CASE SEVENFOLD

Use this to turn collected intakes into a filing. Two audiences, two formats.

---

## Format 1 — federal comment docket (HHS-OASH-2026-0232)

Insert as a new numbered section in the OASH comment. Suggested placement: after
section 4 (documented harm), before section 5.

> ### N. FIRSTHAND ACCOUNTS OF DEPENDENCE ONSET
>
> The Department's own assessment states that "a well-designed human abuse
> potential study has not been conducted that would show whether kratom,
> mitragynine, or 7-OH produce rewarding effects." This Request for Information
> is therefore the appropriate place to submit direct user experience.
>
> We collected **[N]** structured accounts from individuals who used
> 7-OH-containing products. Collection used a non-leading instrument; the
> questionnaire is available to the Department on request. **These accounts are
> not a study and cannot establish a population rate. We do not present them as
> one.** They are offered as direct evidence on questions the record does not
> otherwise address.
>
> Of the [N] accounts:
> - **[X] reported no prior regular opioid use** before the product in question.
> - **[X] reported symptoms on missed or delayed doses.** Reported onset ranged
>   from [range], with a median of approximately [median] of beginning use.
> - **[X] described withdrawal symptoms consistent with opioid withdrawal**
>   [list the recurring ones].
> - **[X] reported resuming the product specifically to stop those symptoms.**
> - **[X] products carried no stated 7-OH content on the label.**
>
> Representative account, identifying details removed, quoted with consent:
>
> > "[quote]"
>
> **REQUESTED ACTION:** The Department has stated that no human abuse potential
> study exists. We ask that it either commission one before the threshold is
> finalised, or state on the record what evidence it is relying on regarding
> dependence liability in users with no prior opioid exposure.

**Rules for this section, non-negotiable:**
- Always give the denominator.
- Never write "most users" or any population claim.
- Never include an account whose respondent did not tick the docket box in H.
- The researcher's own account (Finding 13) is disclosed separately as the
  researcher's, or omitted. It is never folded in anonymously.

## Format 2 — Senate Finance Committee (Wyden inquiry, due 2026-08-31)

Committee staff take sourced material and handle names properly. This can carry
more detail than the public docket.

> **Re: Inquiry into kratom industry influence on federal policy**
>
> Obsidian Watch Group has collected [N] structured firsthand accounts from users
> of 7-OH products, and separately documented the lobbying and financial record
> summarised below. We can make the underlying material available to committee
> staff, including identified accounts where the respondent has consented to
> that.
>
> Points the Committee may find relevant:
> 1. Disclosed kratom lobbying totals **$7,397,500**, of which **$980,000** runs
>    through Miller Strategies for an entity called Botanicals for Better Health
>    and Wellness whose funders are not public, and **$990,000** through Holland &
>    Knight for two clients including a Kratom Trade Association that appears in
>    no public reporting.
> 2. FDA's scientific assessment supporting the 7-OH threshold cites a human
>    pharmacokinetics study **funded by Johnson Foods LLC**, a $700,000 federal
>    lobbying client, and **authored entirely by that company's paid consultants**.
> 3. FDA's assessment attributes the operative "less than 0.05% by weight" figure
>    to **"Kruegel et al., 2019," which does not appear in its own bibliography.**
> 4. The comment period on the threshold was extended on 2026-08-26 **at the
>    request of a party the notice does not name.**
> 5. Firsthand accounts indicate physical dependence in users **with no prior
>    opioid dependence**, at onset intervals of [range].

## Per-account record format (internal)

```
ACCOUNT:      SF-A__
RECEIVED:     YYYY-MM-DD
CONSENT:      [internal | aggregate | quotes | docket | congress | named]
PRODUCT:      brand / form / labelled content or "none stated"
DURATION:     total period of use
PRIOR OPIOID: no / past / current / declined
ONSET:        interval from first use to first missed-dose symptoms
SYMPTOMS:     as described
WITHDRAWAL:   duration, severity, medical care sought
RESUMED:      yes/no, to relieve symptoms yes/no
OTHER SUBST:  as stated, or declined
CORROBORATION: photo / receipt / none
RELIABILITY:  one line, plain
NOTES:
```
