Three things that make this bigger than one product:

1. The federal scheduling action reaches four compounds, all on the mitragynine
   scaffold. The compound class these products are built on is not covered, and
   nothing in the rule brings it in later. On the day 7-OH becomes Schedule I, the
   substitute is still legal.

2. "Legal in all 50 states" appears inaccurate. MGM-15 is Schedule I under Utah law
   and Utah restricted non-leaf kratom products this year.

3. The public comment period on the federal docket closes 10 September.
