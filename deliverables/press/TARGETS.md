## Approach notes

- **One outlet at a time, exclusive on the strongest item.** Do not blast all five.
- **Lead with Buzzers.** It has a quote, a brand, a state agency and a death. It is
  the only item that is a story on its own with no context required.
- **Give them the correction record early.** Three published self-corrections is the
  fastest way to establish you are not an advocate with a thesis.
- **Do not pitch the money first.** It is the weakest hook for a general reader and
  the easiest to dismiss as partisan.
- **The deadline is real leverage:** the comment period closes **2026-09-10**.
