Buzzers FAQ

What are Proprietary Botanical Extracts?

The Proprietary blend of botanical extracts are natural occurring potent compounds that are mean to replicate head changing trips found in our other products found here at ExoClub.

How many Buzzers should I take?

We recommend taking half of a tablet to establish tolerance. Wait about an hour or two before deciding to take more. Any Edible compound take time to metabolize. Therefore, there’s a delay from the time you consume the compound from when it kicks in.

Do Buzzers have Kratom or Kratom Extracts?

No, Buzzers do not contain any kratom or kratom-derived extracts. While they’re designed to deliver a vibe that’s similar to popular kratom products, they are completely kratom-free. Instead, we use a proprietary blend of botanical ingredients that are crafted to give you a clean, functional experience.

What do Buzzers feel like?

Buzzers deliver a smooth, uplifting experience using a proprietary blend of botanical extracts. Formulated to mimic the vibe of popular Kratom products, these tablets are completely free from Kratom or its compounds. Instead, Buzzers use carefully selected botanicals to support clarity, focus, and high-functioning energy.

What is Cat’s Claw?

Cat’s claw is a woody tropical vine native to the Amazon rainforest in South America. It gets its name from the sharp, hooked thorns that look like a cat’s claw. Inside the plant, there’s a natural compound called an indole alkaloid, which can be extracted and used for its unique, energizing properties without using any Kratom.
