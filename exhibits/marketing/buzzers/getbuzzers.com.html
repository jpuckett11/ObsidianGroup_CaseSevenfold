What Is Cat’s Claw?

Cat’s Claw is a climbing vine native to the Amazon, prized for its sharp, curved thorns and natural botanical properties. After much research, we figured out a way to extract an indole alkaloid from the Cat’s Claw plant to mirror the effects of popular kratom products. We’re able to get so close because, despite not using kratom, we’re still producing a very similar indole alkaloid. The results are a non-kratom product with kratom-like effects.
