What are Buzzers?

Buzzers are proprietary botanical extract products. Built around an extracted indole alkaloid from Cat's Claw (Uncaria tomentosa), Buzzers are the modern, formulated answer for adults who want a botanical experience that's legal in all 50 states.

The Buzzers lineup comes in three flavored formats—chewable tablets, larger-strength bottles, and "Homiez"—all engineered for consistency, easy dosing, and pleasant flavor.

At The Green Dragon CBD, we're proud to carry Buzzers because they hit a sweet spot for our customers: a legal, transparent, lab-formulated product that delivers a noticeable experience.

​

What is Cat's Claw and How Do Buzzers Work?

Cat's Claw is a climbing vine native to the Amazon rainforest, named for the curved, claw-shaped thorns along its stems. Indigenous communities in South America have used Cat's Claw for centuries as part of their traditional botanical wellness practices.

What makes Buzzers different from a basic Cat's Claw supplement is the brand's proprietary extraction process. The Buzzers team has developed a way to isolate and refine a specific indole alkaloid from the Cat's Claw plant.

​

Why Choose Buzzers?

  • Legal in all 50 states: Cat's Claw is a recognized botanical and is not a controlled substance, so Buzzers ship nationwide.
  • Precision dosing: every tablet contains a measured, consistent amount, so you always know what you're getting. No scooping powders, no guessing.
  • Multiple strengths and formats: chewable tablets (50mg), bottles (500mg), and Homiez (450mg) to match different use cases.
  • Pleasant flavors: Blue Razz, Cherry Limeade, Kiwi Dragon, Mixed Berry, Sour Apple, Strawberry, Cotton Candy, Watermelon, Strawnana, and more.
