Funding Background
●

●

●

Opioid settlement funds are amounts paid by
pharmaceutical manufacturers, distributors, and
pharmacies to state and local governments to resolve
lawsuits over their role in the national opioid crisis.
These funds are intended exclusively for opioid
remediation eﬀorts, including treatment, prevention,
and recovery programs. Approved uses are outlined
in the One Utah Opioid Settlement MOU.
The funds are allocated through the Social Services
Appropriations Committee with recommendations
from the Opioid Task Force and the Opioid Settlement
Advisory Committee.

Funding Updates
●

●

●

●

●

Passed during the 2025 General Session, HB 10 reclassiﬁes the
state’s opioid settlement funding and restricts how those funds
may be appropriated.
The Governor’s FY27 budget recommends $1,600,000 of one-time
funding be used from this fund for opioid remediation projects.
This represents a reduction in funding: In FY24, $7,061,033 of
ongoing funding was appropriated to opioid remediation
programs. This funding will expire at the end of FY26.
The transition to the new funding structure made into law by
HB10 and the governor's budget recommendation requires a
strategic choice between broad allocation and targeted allocation.
Our oﬃce recommends targeted allocation toward three
high performing programs that serve people across Utah.
